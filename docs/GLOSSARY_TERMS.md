## Writing Standard

Each term includes:

- `key`
- `term`
- `short_definition`
- `long_definition`
- `example`
- `common_misunderstanding`
- `related_terms`

Definitions should stay practical, beginner-readable, and transparent about limitations. Terms that relate to modeling or interpretation should avoid causal claims unless the study design supports them.
